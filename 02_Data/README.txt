POWER BI HR WORKFORCE & ATTRITION ANALYTICS
Synthetic portfolio dataset for Power BI practice.

Employees: employee master data (with a few intentional blanks and duplicate rows for Power Query practice).
Departments, Locations, Roles: dimensions.
Performance: annual employee performance records.
Attendance: monthly attendance summaries.
Date: calendar dimension.

Coverage:
Employee joins: 2017-2026
Performance: 2024-2026
Attendance: Jan 2025-Sep 2026

We will practice Power Query, data modeling, star schema, relationships, DAX,
filter context, time intelligence, interactive dashboards, Power BI Service concepts,
and interview questions.

The data is synthetic and must not be presented as data from a real company.
